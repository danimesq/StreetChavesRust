## Conclusions

- Both SDL references model the original 15-character roster.
- Pascal C16 is Chapolin, documented as generated by `GeraChapolin`.
- C's `osc_frame_offset[16][64]` includes a zero/index row and does not establish 16 fighters.
- Keep Chapolin optional until game-mode availability and asset provenance are reviewed. The plan is to preserve it as a recoverable **2026 bonus** rather than fold it into the original roster. See [Chapolin bonus plan](chapolin-bonus-2026.md).
